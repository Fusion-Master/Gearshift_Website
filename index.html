What is FTC

Robotics competition, real engineering

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is an international robotics competition for students in grades 7–12. Each season brings a brand-new game, and teams like ours design, build, wire, and program a robot from scratch to compete head-to-head.

Beyond the robot, FTC pushes us to think like engineers — managing a season-long project, keeping an engineering notebook, and presenting our work to judges alongside driving on the field.

It's more than a competition: it's where we learn CAD, coding, electrical wiring, and teamwork that carries into everything we do.
